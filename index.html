How it works

Type like you always do.

MSP only watches one key. Everything else you press reaches the app exactly as Windows delivers it.

  1. 01

    Write the sentence.

    Type to the end of a thought the way you already do. One tap of the space bar is still a plain space.

  2. 02

    Tap space twice.

    MSP catches the second tap and types the period where the full stop belongs, then adds the space after it.

  3. 03

    Keep writing.

    The next sentence starts the moment your hands do. The app stays in the notification area until you need it.

What it restores

One gesture, done properly.

MSP watches the space bar and nothing else. Here is the whole of it — and the Windows habit each part replaces.

  • Gesture What MSP does On Windows today
  • space ×2

    A full stop, where the sentence ends.

    The second tap is the trigger, and the period lands in that exact position — the end of what you have just typed.

    On Windows today No equivalent exists. You reach for . on the top row, or the numpad .
  • . + space

    The trailing space arrives with it.

    The full stop and the space after it are one keystroke, so the next sentence starts without a second reach.

    On Windows today Two separate keys: . then Space
  • space ×1

    One tap is still a space.

    Only a second tap inside a short window counts. Every other key you press reaches Windows exactly as it does today.

    On Windows today Unchanged — Space still means Space
  • any app
