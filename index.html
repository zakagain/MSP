How it works

Type like you always do.

MSP only watches one key. Everything else you press reaches the app exactly as Windows delivers it.

  1. 01

    Write the sentence.

    Type to the end of a thought the way you already do. One tap of the space bar is still a plain space.

  2. 02

    Tap space twice.

    MSP catches the second tap and types the period where the full stop belongs, then adds the space after it.

  3. 03

    Keep writing.

    The next sentence starts the moment your hands do. The app stays in the notification area until you need it.
